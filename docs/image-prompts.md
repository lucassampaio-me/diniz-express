# Prompts de Imagem - Hero Diniz Express

Este documento reúne prompts prontos para gerar a imagem principal do hero da landing page da Diniz Express em dois geradores:

- ChatGPT Image
- Google AI Studio / Nano Banana Pro

Objetivo da imagem:

- retratar um motoboy realista de Sao Paulo
- posicionar o personagem mais a direita
- manter espaco negativo a esquerda para headline e CTA
- usar jaqueta alinhada a paleta da marca
- trocar mochila de delivery por bau rigido traseiro
- mostrar ao fundo os utilitarios da operacao de forma sutil
- manter ambiente claramente diurno

Paleta de referencia da marca:

- `#0B1E36`
- `#0D2B4E`
- `#0F3460`
- `#1565C0`
- `#1E88E5`

Observacoes importantes:

- evitar vermelho na jaqueta
- evitar visual de app de delivery
- evitar cara de banco de imagem
- evitar poluicao visual e excesso de elementos
- evitar aparencia noturna ou de fim de tarde escuro
- preferir proporcao `16:9`

## ChatGPT Image - English

```text
Create a photorealistic website hero image for a Brazilian delivery company.

Scene: a real-looking motoboy from Sao Paulo on a motorcycle, positioned on the right side of the image. He should look like a genuine working-class courier from the city, everyday and believable, not like a stock model. He should have a friendly, approachable expression with a light natural smile. He wears a dark navy-black work jacket with a subtle blue undertone, matching the Diniz Express palette, with restrained accents in brand blue. The jacket should feel professional and understated, not sporty. The motorcycle has a hard rear cargo box, common among Sao Paulo couriers, instead of a delivery backpack.

Background: clean, premium, minimal, in a daytime environment with soft natural daylight. Keep large empty space on the left for headline and CTA text, but do not add dark vignette, artificial shadow blocks or heavy overlays on that side. In the background, include a subtle lineup of utility vehicles used by the company: a white Fiorino-style van, a white HR or Ducato-style van, and another compact urban cargo utility vehicle. They should appear softly in the distance, secondary to the motoboy.

Style: realistic commercial photography, Brazilian urban logistics, trustworthy, professional, premium but grounded in real life. The image should feel like Sao Paulo, not futuristic and not Americanized. Clean composition, soft depth of field, realistic lighting, high detail, landing-page-ready. The scene must be clearly daytime, with bright but controlled natural light, neutral tonal balance, and no night-time mood.

Avoid: red jacket, night scene, sunset scene, blue-hour scene, food delivery backpack, iFood branding, app branding, race-bike styling, studio-white background, exaggerated smiles, artificial beauty ad look, cluttered street scene, heavy traffic, text, logos, watermark, left-side shadow overlay, dark vignette.

Aspect ratio: 16:9
```

## Ajustes Rapidos

Se a imagem sair publicitaria demais:

```text
make the rider look more documentary-real and less like a polished advertising model
```

Se a imagem sair generica ou gringa:

```text
strong Brazilian Sao Paulo urban courier identity, local realism, practical delivery motorcycle common in Brazil
```

Se os utilitarios roubarem muita atencao:

```text
keep the utility vehicles subtle, distant and secondary, with the motoboy as the clear focal point
```

Se a imagem ficar azul demais:

```text
reduce blue dominance, keep the jacket navy-black and preserve neutral tonal balance
```

Se a imagem tender para noite:

```text
make the scene clearly daytime with soft natural daylight, neutral sky brightness, and no night or dusk atmosphere
```
